# Code of Conduct

Agent Workflow adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. This page summarizes its standards and explains how to report concerns in this project. [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)

## Our pledge

We commit to a welcoming community where people can participate without harassment. This commitment applies across age, appearance, body size, caste, color, disability, education, ethnicity, experience, gender identity or expression, nationality, race, religion, sex characteristics, sexual identity or orientation, and socioeconomic status.

## Our standards

Treat other contributors with kindness. Respect different experiences and viewpoints, give and receive constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes, and consider the welfare of the whole community.

Conduct outside these standards includes sexual advances or attention, sexualized language or imagery, trolling, personal or political attacks, harassment in public or private, and disclosure of someone's private information without their permission. Other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting is also unacceptable.

## Scope and moderation

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public, including at events or through official accounts. Community leaders explain the standards, investigate reports promptly and fairly, and safeguard the reporter's privacy and security. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate the policy and explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting

Contact a repository maintainer through a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If no private method is listed, ask for a private channel without sharing incident details or identifying anyone. Keep reports out of public issues and pull requests.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders choose a response that reflects the conduct and its impact.

1. **Correction**. For unwelcome or unprofessional conduct, a private written notice explains the violation and may request a public apology.
2. **Warning**. For a violation arising from one or more incidents, a formal warning may prohibit contact with affected people and enforcement leaders for a specified period, including outside project spaces. Breaching those terms can lead to a ban.
3. **Temporary ban**. For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate conduct, the person may be excluded from project participation and barred from contacting those involved for a set period. Breaching those terms can lead to permanent exclusion.
4. **Permanent ban**. An ongoing pattern of violations, targeted harassment, or aggression or disparagement directed at a group may lead to permanent exclusion from the community.
